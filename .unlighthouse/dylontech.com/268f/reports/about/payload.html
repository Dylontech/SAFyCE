About Me

Hi, I'm Dylon Gabriel Mandujano Gonzalez.

I am a young web developer and systems engineering student, I am 22 years old and was born in Mexico City.

I have always been passionate about technology and I am excited to see how it advances every day. My interest in computers began thanks to a great friend, who showed me that a PC could be much more than Word and Excel: it could be a tool to create art.

That's how I discovered FL Studio and started experimenting with music production. Although the program was intuitive, I soon wanted to install plugins to expand it... and, like any beginner, I downloaded some files from questionable sources. My first laptop—a humble i3 with 8GB of RAM and Windows 8.1—ended up completely infected with malware.

This problem sparked my curiosity: I wanted to understand why it happened and how to fix it. My first big challenge was formatting my computer, with a little help from my computer science teacher. Since then, I value digital security a lot and always recommend using original software or, if not possible, looking for free and reliable alternatives.

After many adventures (like taking apart my own PC, panicking because it wouldn't turn on, and putting it back together better than before), my love for technology and learning only grew.

Today I am about to graduate from university and I am working to build my portfolio, share my projects, and continue growing as a professional and idea creator.

Welcome to my space!
